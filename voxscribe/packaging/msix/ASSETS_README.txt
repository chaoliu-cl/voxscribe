MSIX visual assets required (PNG)

Place the following files in packaging\msix\assets:
- StoreLogo.png (50x50)
- Square44x44Logo.png (44x44)
- Square71x71Logo.png (71x71)
- Square150x150Logo.png (150x150)
- Square310x310Logo.png (310x310)
- Wide310x150Logo.png (310x150)

Microsoft Store listings also require higher-resolution artwork and screenshots,
but those are uploaded in Partner Center, not in the MSIX package.
